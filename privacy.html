Privacy Policy

NFL Collection Pilot · Updated September 15, 2026

This policy describes the intended operation of NFL Collection Pilot, a personal research application maintained by GitHub account nbabird21. The collector is in setup; publishing this policy does not mean it has been deployed. Its implementation must match this policy before Google account access is used.

Information accessed and collected

The collector uses permitted NFL data and sportsbook observations, including event identifiers, lines, prices, source timestamps and collection-health logs. Google authorization supplies OAuth credentials needed to access the owner's selected project files.

The requested Google Drive permission is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permits creation and management of files created by the app, or explicitly shared with or selected for the app. The app is not intended to request unrestricted Drive access or access to Gmail, contacts or calendar. Operational records may include Google file identifiers and file metadata needed to locate, validate and recover the research files.

Purpose and processing

Google access is used only to store, retrieve, update and recover the personal research files and their collection history. The collector is designed to process this information in the owner's private GitHub Actions environment and store persistent research data in the owner's Google Drive. One-time authorization may also occur on the owner's computer.

Google user data will not be sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. The collection pilot does not send private Google file contents to an AI service. Any later feature that materially changes data use must be disclosed before it is enabled.

Service providers and disclosure

Google and GitHub process information necessary to provide Drive, authorization, hosting and runner services under their own terms and policies. Odds and football-data providers receive requests necessary to retrieve their data; the collector is not intended to send them the owner's private Drive contents or Google authorization tokens.

Private collection records are not published on this website. Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law. The app's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Storage and security

The intended setup keeps credentials in private local authorization files or GitHub Actions secrets, not public repositories, website pages, spreadsheet cells or logs. Research files remain in the owner's private storage. Access must be limited to the owner and services necessary to operate the collector. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Research history is retained until the owner deletes it or configures a documented retention policy. The owner can revoke Google access in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not delete existing files, local credentials, runner artifacts or backups.

To remove stored information, the owner can stop collection schedules, revoke access, remove repository secrets and local credential files, delete the project files and backups from Drive, empty Drive trash, and remove any retained private runner artifacts. Service-provider backup and retention rules may also apply.

Public website

These pages contain no app-added analytics, advertising, forms or tracking scripts. The hosting provider may process routine connection information such as IP addresses and request logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy for their practices.

Changes and contact

Changes will be posted here with an updated date. Questions may be submitted through the project issue tracker. It is public: never include credentials, personal account details or private research data.
