NFL Collection Pilot

NFL Collection Pilot is a personal NFL research tool maintained by the GitHub account nbabird21. Its purpose is to collect permitted football data and sportsbook observations, preserve timestamped history, and monitor source freshness and collection failures.

What the pilot is designed to do

  • Collect available odds and football performance data through authorized sources.
  • Store research files, collection logs and recovery checkpoints in the owner's private Google Drive.
  • Run scheduled collection through a private GitHub repository, subject to configured usage limits.

The collector is being configured. This public site provides app information; it does not indicate that collection has been deployed or that any live integration has passed testing.

Why Google access is requested

The app requests the limited Google Drive drive.file permission to create and manage its own research files, and files explicitly selected for it. It is not intended to request unrestricted access to the owner's Drive, Gmail, contacts or calendar.

Personal use and boundaries

This pilot is for its owner's use, not a public subscription service. It does not place wagers or enable automated pricing or staking. Its public website contains no account credentials or private research records.
